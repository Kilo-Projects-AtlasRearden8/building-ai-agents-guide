# What an AI Agent Is (and Isn't)

Welcome. This manual is for anyone who has heard the phrase "AI agent" and wondered what it actually means. No coding experience needed. No math needed. Just curiosity.

## An agent, in plain English

An AI agent is an AI that can do more than chat. A normal AI assistant answers questions. An agent takes steps toward a goal — it can look things up, do calculations, write drafts, send messages, or move files, then look at the results and decide what to do next.

Think of the difference this way:

- **Assistant:** You ask "What's the weather?" It tells you.
- **Agent:** You say "Find me a hotel under $150 near the conference, then draft an email to my team with the options." It checks prices, compares them, and writes the email — checking with you before it books anything.

## The four ideas in this guide

This manual covers four building blocks, plus one way to connect agents to the tools they need:

1. **Planning** — breaking a goal into small steps
2. **Tool use** — letting the agent act (search, calculate, write)
3. **Memory** — remembering what happened before
4. **Safety** — keeping the agent helpful and harmless
5. **MCP overview** — a simple way for agents to plug into tools and data

## No coding required to start

You can design an agent with just paper and a pen. The worksheet at the end of this manual walks you through it. Coding comes later, only if you want it.

## A quick word on safety

Agents act on your behalf, so they need guardrails. This guide is education only — never medical advice, never financial advice. Always keep a human in the loop before an agent does anything consequential (sending email, spending money, changing records).
